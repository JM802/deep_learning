# Mission

## Topic

Deep learning fundamentals from linear models to convolutional neural networks.

## Why This Matters

The learner wants to review the first seven chapters of their deep learning course, especially the path from linear regression and softmax regression through MLPs, regularization, deep learning computation, basic CNNs, and modern CNN architectures.

## Current Goal

Build durable recall and problem-solving ability with high-quality review questions, not just passive reading.

## Success Criteria

- Explain the purpose and mechanics of each major model family.
- Compute tensor shapes, parameter counts, convolution output sizes, and loss/gradient behavior.
- Diagnose common training problems such as overfitting, vanishing gradients, poor initialization, and shape mismatch.
- Compare CNN architectures such as LeNet, AlexNet, VGG, NiN, GoogLeNet, BatchNorm networks, and ResNet.
